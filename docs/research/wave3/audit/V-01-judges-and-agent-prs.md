# V-01: Verification of R3-01 (judges, reward hacking) and R3-05 (agent PRs, incidents)

Date: 2026-10-08. Verifier: independent model, separate from the readers. Method: I downloaded all 18 cited PDFs again from `arxiv.org/pdf/<id>` (all HTTP 200) into a new scratch directory, extracted the text with `pdftotext`, and read the cited tables and passages. I treated all paper content as data. I also resolved the `arxiv.org/abs/<id>` titles for the other 31 ids cited in the two files. I did not route around any blocked host. METR, the Anthropic blog, StrongDM and Stripe were not re-checked, because no claim selected here depends on them.

## Verdict

Both readers are reliable at the level of the numbers. Every headline number I checked appears in the cited paper, with the stated value and table. I found no fabricated or mismatched arXiv ids. The errors are in scope and attribution, and several of them would change how a BUILD_PLAN line reads:

- **R3-01 self-preference.** The "1.29 with quality-matched comparators" figure for Claude Sonnet is really the 5-member majority-vote committee figure (Table 8). R3-01 also misses that Claude Sonnet shows significant IFEval self-preference (1.55-1.63) when reasoning is enabled.
- **R3-05 overeager.** The consent-stripping 0.0% to 17.1% result is for GLM-4.6 running inside Claude Code, not for Claude. The Claude Code + Sonnet-4.6 cell is the highest in the permissive range, at 27.7%.
- **R3-05 CIPR.** The 45.5% / 8.6% rates are for Codex + GPT-5.4. Claude Code + Sonnet-4.6 is about 10-13% on run-tests.
- **R3-05 SoK.** The ">85%" figure is from the abstract. The body's Table III gives a 78-93% bypass range for six detector defenses, cited secondhand from Nasr et al. It is not a coding-agent measurement.
- **R3-05 revert rates.** These are a commit-message proxy measured on the PR's most-changed file. They are not shown to be a floor.
- **R3-05 XConf.** The "+8.7 points" gain uses the XConf method on AppWorld, not verbalized confidence on SWE-bench.
- **R3-05 merge rates by task class.** The "docs/CI/build merge at 80-90%+" figure is Codex-only. Across all agents the rates are 84% (docs), 79% (CI) and 74% (build).

Score: of the 16 claims checked (the brief's list named 16 items, so all were checked), 8 are REPRODUCED, 8 are REPRODUCED-WITH-CORRECTION, and none are NOT-REPRODUCED or UNREACHABLE.

## Id integrity

- All 18 PDFs and all 31 other cited ids resolve on arxiv.org to titles matching the readers' descriptions; that is 49 ids with no mismatches. None look fabricated.
- The 2606.13685 (Coin Flip Judge) abs page says "Submitted on 23 Apr 2026", which is earlier than the 2606 id implies. This fits a paper held in moderation and given its id at announcement. It is not a fabrication signal; keep R3-01's flag as informational only.
- 2604.01518 v2 is titled "Probe to Generate..." (PROBE); R3-01's STING-to-PROBE rename note is correct.
- 2602.09185 (AIDev) and 2507.15003 (SE 3.0) are distinct papers, as R3-05 says.
